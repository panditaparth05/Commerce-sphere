The Problem

Fragmented data creates fragmented customer experiences.

Customer events are scattered across website analytics, app behavior, CRM, orders, support tickets, campaigns, wishlist activity, and cart sessions. Teams operate without a unified profile and personalization suffers.

Our Solution

A complete customer intelligence layer built for commerce.

CommerceSphere resolves identities, unifies behaviors, scores customer value, predicts churn, and powers next-best-action recommendations across every customer touchpoint.

Customer 360°

One profile for identity, orders, preferences, engagement, and lifecycle signals.

AI Intelligence

Predict churn, detect high-value users, and explain model decisions with transparency.

Personalization

Activate segments, recommendations, and campaigns using real customer context.

Analytics

Track revenue, retention, RFM segments, customer growth, and lifecycle performance.

Architecture

Ingestion pipelines, identity resolution, customer graph, AI models, campaign connectors, and a secure activation layer designed for scale.

Benefits

Retain more customers, personalize smarter, improve conversion, and make faster data-driven decisions.

Future Scope

Real-time next-best-action orchestration, omnichannel journey optimization, and deeper explainable AI workflows.
