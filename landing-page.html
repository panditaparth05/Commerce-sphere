The Problem

Customer data is scattered across channels, teams, and systems.

Commerce teams often operate with fragmented order data, disconnected engagement signals, and incomplete customer identities. That makes personalization weak, retention reactive, and analytics unreliable.

Fragmented profiles

Orders, web behavior, CRM interactions, marketing responses, and support history live in separate tools.

Limited predictive intelligence

Without unified signals, brands struggle to identify churn risk, predict spend, and surface next-best actions.

Personalization gaps

Generic campaigns and untimely recommendations lead to lower conversion, weaker loyalty, and reduced lifetime value.
